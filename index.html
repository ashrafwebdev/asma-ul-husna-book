About the book

Ninety-nine names. Ninety-nine ways to draw near.

Each of Allah's Beautiful Names carries its own meaning and its own door — a way of understanding Him, and a way of shaping how we live. This book walks through all 99 names one by one: what each name means, what scholars and tradition say about the benefit of reciting it, and how to bring that remembrance into daily life.

Written in plain language for readers with no prior background in Arabic or Islamic studies, while staying grounded in traditional sources.

الرَّحْمَٰن

Ar-Rahman

The Most Compassionate

Recited as a reminder that Allah's mercy reaches every creation before anything is asked of Him. Tradition ties this name to softening the heart and finding comfort in hardship.

الرَّزَّاق

Ar-Razzaq

The Provider

Recited when seeking sustenance or relief from worry over provision. Reflecting on this name is traditionally linked to contentment and trust in what has already been apportioned.

الْوَدُود

Al-Wadud

The Most Loving

Recited to soften relationships and increase goodwill between people. Scholars often connect this name to reconciliation and the mending of strained ties.
